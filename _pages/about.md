I am an incoming PhD student with a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Data Science from California State Polytechnic University, Pomona (expected Dec 2025). I work as a visiting student in **Dr. Yusu Wang’s lab** at UC San Diego on graph neural networks, structure-aware modeling, and interpretability, and I have collaborated with **Dr. Tingting Chen** at Cal Poly Pomona on multimodal motion and mobility analysis.

My research interests sit at the intersection of **geometric deep learning**, **graph-based models**, and **interpretability**: how to represent and generate relational structure, how to learn graph-structured outputs from unordered or high-dimensional data, and how methods such as the average gradient outer product (AGOP) can reveal what GNNs rely on when they reason over irregular data. I am motivated by scientific and applied settings where noise and hidden relationships make transparent, structure-aware models especially valuable.

Previously, I was an NSF-sponsored undergraduate research assistant on multimodal mobility-scooter analysis, a machine learning engineer intern at Character Face Generation working on 3D mesh modeling, and a Cal-Bridge Scholar. I am a co-author on work at IEEE BigData and the AAAI Symposium Series and am preparing submissions building on AGOP for GNNs and Set2Graph-style structure learning.
